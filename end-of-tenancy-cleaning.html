End of tenancy cleaning

A thorough clean when a tenant moves out, so the property or unit is ready to hand back or let again. For landlords, letting agents, housing associations, tenants and businesses at the end of a lease.

Request a free quote Book a free site visit

What does an end of tenancy clean cover?

An end of tenancy clean is a full clean of an empty property or unit when a tenancy or lease ends. We clean every room top to bottom, including inside the kitchen appliances and cupboards, the bathrooms, floors, skirting boards, doors and internal windows. We agree the list with you first, and arrange access with you or your agent.

What's included

We agree the exact list with you. An end of tenancy clean usually includes:

  • Kitchen: oven, hob, extractor, fridge, freezer and cupboards inside and out
  • Bathrooms and toilets descaled and cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Skirting boards, doors and door frames
  • Light switches, sockets and radiators
  • Internal windows and glass
  • Commercial units cleaned throughout, ready to hand back

Who we clean for

Landlords and letting agents between tenants, housing associations with empty homes to turn around, tenants moving out, and businesses handing back an office, shop or unit at the end of a lease.

Access and timing

We arrange access with you, your tenant or your agent, and agree a date that fits your move-out and handover.

End of tenancy cleaning: common questions

Do you clean for tenants as well as landlords?

Yes. We clean for tenants moving out, landlords, letting agents and housing associations.

Do you clean empty homes for housing associations?

Yes. We clean empty homes between tenancies so they are ready for the next tenant.

Do you clean commercial units at the end of a lease?

Yes. We clean offices, shops and units so they are ready to hand back to the landlord.

Do I need to be there?

No. We can arrange access with you, your tenant or your letting agent.

How much does it cost?

Every building is different, so we do not have a price list. We visit for free, agree exactly what needs doing and how often, then send you a written quotation. There is no obligation, and we are not VAT registered, so there is no VAT to add.
