Our local areas

We focus on the areas closest to our Northwood base: Northwood, Ruislip, Pinner, Eastcote, Harrow, Rickmansworth and Watford.

  • Northwood

    Northwood is home. We are based in HA6, so the offices, shops, halls and blocks of flats around Green Lane, Northwood Hills and Moor Park are right on our doorstep.

  • Ruislip

    Regular cleaning for offices, shops, halls and blocks of flats in Ruislip, Ruislip Manor, South Ruislip and Eastcote, from a cleaning company just down the road in Northwood.
