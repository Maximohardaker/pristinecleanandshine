Services

What we clean

High-end regular cleaning contracts for commercial and shared buildings. Every clean is done to deep clean quality by experienced, managed cleaners. The lists below are a starting point. We agree the exact work with you, then give you your price in a quotation.

Offices

We agree cleaning times with you, so the work does not get in the way of the working day.

  • Desks and work surfaces
  • Floors, vacuumed and mopped
  • Kitchens and tea points
  • Toilets and washrooms
  • Meeting rooms and reception
  • Internal glass and door handles
  • Bins emptied

Shops

Customers see the floor and the glass first. We plan visits around your opening hours, so the shop is ready when the doors open.

  • Shop floors
  • Entrance doors and internal glass
  • Shelving and display surfaces
  • Fitting rooms
  • Staff rooms and kitchens
  • Toilets
  • Bins emptied

Community halls

A hall can have several groups through it in a week. We time visits around your bookings, so the room is ready for the next one.

  • Main hall floors
  • Kitchens
  • Toilets
  • Entrances and foyers
  • Stages and side rooms
  • Tables and chairs wiped down
  • Bins emptied

Churches

Churches often have old floors and wooden furniture, and they are in use for services and events through the week. We agree a routine that fits around them.

  • Naves and aisles
  • Pews and chairs
  • Vestries
  • Porches and entrances
  • Church halls and meeting rooms
  • Kitchens and toilets

Communal areas of blocks of flats

For managing agents, freeholders and residents' associations. Shared areas get a lot of use, and they are the first thing a visitor sees.

  • Stairwells and landings
  • Corridors
  • Entrance halls and doors
  • Handrails and light switches
  • Bin stores

Our contracts

Flexible, with no long tie-in. Your price and terms are set out in your quotation.

  • Early morning, evening, daytime or weekend cleaning
  • A named contact for any questions
  • Cover when a cleaner is off sick or on holiday
  • Washroom supplies kept topped up
  • Occasional deep cleans for contract customers
