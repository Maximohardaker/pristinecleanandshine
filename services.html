Commercial cleaning services

Regular cleaning contracts for offices, shops, church and community halls and the communal areas of blocks of flats, plus deep cleans and end of tenancy cleans. Every regular clean is done to deep clean quality, and every job is priced by free quotation.

Office cleaning

A clean, fresh office every morning, without you having to think about it. We clean before you open, after you close or during the day, on a contract that suits you.

More about office cleaning

  • Desks and work surfaces wiped
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Kitchens and tea points cleaned
  • Toilets and washrooms cleaned, with soap and paper topped up
  • Meeting rooms and reception

Shop and retail cleaning

Customers notice the floor, the glass and the fitting rooms first. We clean around your opening hours so your shop is ready when the doors open.

More about shop and retail cleaning

  • Shop floors vacuumed, swept and mopped
  • Entrance doors and internal glass
  • Shelving, counters and display surfaces
  • Fitting rooms
  • Staff rooms and kitchens

Church and community hall cleaning

Halls and churches are busy places, with different groups in every day of the week. We time our cleans around your services, bookings and events, so the space is ready for whoever comes next.

More about church and community hall cleaning

  • Main hall floors
  • Naves, aisles and pews
  • Vestries, side rooms and meeting rooms
  • Stages
  • Kitchens

Communal area cleaning

Stairwells, corridors and entrance halls get heavy use, and they are the first thing residents and visitors see. We keep them clean on a regular schedule.

More about communal area cleaning

  • Entrance halls and entrance doors
  • Stairwells and landings
  • Corridors
  • Handrails, light switches and door handles
  • Internal glass

Deep cleaning

A thorough top-to-bottom clean that reaches the places a regular clean does not. Ideal before a new cleaning contract starts, or once or twice a year to reset a busy building.

More about deep cleaning

  • Kitchen deep clean: ovens, microwaves, fridges and cupboards inside and out
  • Washrooms descaled and fully cleaned
  • Skirting boards, doors and door frames
  • Radiators, light switches and sockets
  • High and low dusting

End of tenancy cleaning

A thorough clean when a tenant moves out, so the property or unit is ready to hand back or let again. For landlords, letting agents, housing associations, tenants and businesses at the end of a lease.

More about end of tenancy cleaning

  • Kitchen: oven, hob, extractor, fridge, freezer and cupboards inside and out
  • Bathrooms and toilets descaled and cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Skirting boards, doors and door frames
  • Light switches, sockets and radiators
