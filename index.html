Regular cleaning contracts for businesses and shared buildings.

You tell us how often the building needs cleaning, and we agree what gets done on each visit. Every regular clean is done to deep clean quality. Contracts are flexible, with no long tie-in, and we can clean in the early morning, in the evening, during the day or at weekends. Every building is different, so we do not have a set price list. Your price comes in a quotation, after we have spoken to you or seen the site.

What we clean

  • Offices

    Desks, kitchens, toilets, meeting rooms and floors.

  • Shops

    Shop floors, glass, fitting rooms, staff areas and toilets.

  • Community halls

    Main halls, kitchens, toilets, entrances and side rooms.

  • Churches

    Naves, pews, vestries, church halls and toilets.

  • Communal areas of flats

    Stairwells, corridors, entrances and bin stores.
