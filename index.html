Regular cleaning for businesses and shared buildings

Pristine Clean & Shine is a commercial cleaning company based in Northwood. We clean offices, shops, church and community halls and the communal areas of blocks of flats on regular contracts, from daily to weekly. We also do deep cleans and end of tenancy cleans.

You choose how often and when, from early mornings to weekends. Contracts are flexible with no long tie-in. There is no price list: we visit for free, then send you a clear written quotation.

Our cleaning services

  • Office cleaning

    Desks, kitchens, washrooms, meeting rooms and floors, before or after hours.

  • Shop and retail cleaning

    Shop floors, glass, displays, fitting rooms and staff areas, around your opening hours.

  • Church and community hall cleaning

    Halls, naves, pews, kitchens and toilets, timed around your bookings.

  • Communal area cleaning

    Stairwells, corridors, entrances and bin stores in blocks of flats.

  • Deep cleaning

    A thorough top-to-bottom clean, including kitchens and washrooms.

  • End of tenancy cleaning

    Homes and commercial units cleaned and ready to let or hand back.
