About Pristine Clean & Shine

A commercial cleaning company based in Northwood, HA6, cleaning offices, shops, halls and blocks of flats across North West London and the Home Counties.

Who we are

Pristine Clean & Shine is the trading name of PRISTINECLEANANDSHINE LTD, a limited company registered in England and Wales. We are based in Northwood and clean commercial and shared buildings on regular contracts.

We clean offices, shops and retail units, churches, church halls and community halls, and the communal areas of blocks of flats. We also do deep cleans and end of tenancy cleans.

How we work

We take on experienced cleaners who are vetted, trusted and reliable, and our supervisors check their work. Every regular clean is done to deep clean quality. You have a named contact, and we arrange cover when a cleaner is off sick or on holiday.

We never work from a price list. Every job starts with a free conversation and a free site visit, then a written quotation, so you know exactly what you are getting.

What the Central Digital Platform registration means

The Central Digital Platform is the UK government's register for suppliers who bid for public sector contracts. Being registered, with a Public Procurement Organisation Number (PPON), means we can quote for work with councils, housing associations and other public bodies.
