Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our cleaning services, quotes, contracts and the areas we cover. Can't find what you need? Get in touch.

Quotes and prices

How much does commercial cleaning cost?

Every building is different, so we price each job individually. We visit for free, agree what needs doing and how often, then send a written quotation. There is no obligation.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. The quote and the site visit are both free, with no obligation to go ahead.

Do you charge VAT?

No. We are not VAT registered, so there is no VAT to add to our prices.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in our quote form, email info@pristinecleanandshine.co.uk or call 07495 595 695.

Services

What buildings do you clean?

Offices, shops and retail units, churches, church halls and community halls, and the communal areas of blocks of flats. We also do deep cleans and end of tenancy cleans.

Do you do one-off cleans?

Yes. We do deep cleans and end of tenancy cleans as one-off jobs. Our main service is regular contract cleaning.

Do you clean windows?

Yes. We can add window cleaning to your regular clean, or include it in a deep clean.

Do you bring your own cleaning materials?

Yes. We bring our own materials and equipment. If you would rather supply your own, you can.

Do you restock washroom supplies?

Yes. We keep washroom supplies such as soap and paper topped up for contract customers.

Contracts and scheduling

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Our contracts are flexible, with no long tie-in. Your terms are set out in your quotation.

When can you clean?

Early mornings, evenings, during the day and at weekends. We fit around your opening hours and bookings.

What if my usual cleaner is off?

We arrange cover when a cleaner is off sick or on holiday, so your cleaning still happens.

Who do I speak to if there is a problem?

You have a named contact for any questions, and our supervisors check the work.

About us

Are you insured?

Yes. We are fully insured.

Are your cleaners vetted?

Yes. Our cleaners are vetted, trusted and reliable, and they are managed by our supervisors.

Are you a registered company?

Yes. Pristine Clean & Shine is the trading name of PRISTINECLEANANDSHINE LTD, company number 16100576, registered in England and Wales.

Can you work on public sector contracts?

Yes. We are registered on the Central Digital Platform with a Public Procurement Organisation Number (PPON).

Where are you based?

We are based in Northwood, HA6, and cover North West London, London, Hertfordshire, Surrey, Berkshire and Buckinghamshire. See the areas we cover.
