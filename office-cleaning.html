Office cleaning in Northwood and North West London

A clean, fresh office every morning, without you having to think about it. We clean before you open, after you close or during the day, on a contract that suits you.

Request a free quote Book a free site visit

What does an office cleaning contract involve?

We agree a cleaning schedule with you, from daily to weekly, and a list of what gets done on each visit. Our vetted, experienced cleaners then come at the times you choose and a supervisor checks the work. Every regular clean is done to deep clean quality. We bring our own materials, and you can supply your own if you prefer.

What's included

A typical office clean covers the list below. We adjust it to your building and agree it with you before we start.

  • Desks and work surfaces wiped
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Kitchens and tea points cleaned
  • Toilets and washrooms cleaned, with soap and paper topped up
  • Meeting rooms and reception
  • Internal glass, door handles and light switches
  • Bins and recycling emptied
  • Window cleaning, if you would like it added

When we clean

Early mornings, evenings, during the working day or at weekends. Most offices like us to finish before staff arrive, but we fit around your hours, your alarm and your access arrangements.

Who it suits

Office managers, business owners and facilities teams who want reliable cleaning on a flexible contract with no long tie-in, and a named contact to speak to.

Office cleaning: common questions

Can you clean outside office hours?

Yes. We clean early in the morning, in the evening and at weekends, as well as during the day.

Do I need to supply cleaning materials?

No. We bring our own materials and equipment. If you would rather use your own products, that is fine too.

What happens if our usual cleaner is off?

We arrange cover, so your office is still cleaned when a cleaner is off sick or on holiday.

How much does it cost?

Every building is different, so we do not have a price list. We visit for free, agree exactly what needs doing and how often, then send you a written quotation. There is no obligation, and we are not VAT registered, so there is no VAT to add.
