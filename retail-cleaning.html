Shop and retail cleaning

Customers notice the floor, the glass and the fitting rooms first. We clean around your opening hours so your shop is ready when the doors open.

Request a free quote Book a free site visit

How does shop cleaning work?

We visit your shop or retail unit for free, agree what needs cleaning and how often, then clean before you open, after you close or on quiet days. Every clean is done to deep clean quality by vetted cleaners, and a supervisor checks the work.

What's included

Every shop is laid out differently. This is what we usually cover, and we agree the final list with you.

  • Shop floors vacuumed, swept and mopped
  • Entrance doors and internal glass
  • Shelving, counters and display surfaces
  • Fitting rooms
  • Staff rooms and kitchens
  • Toilets, with supplies topped up
  • Bins emptied
  • Shop window cleaning, if you would like it added

Around your opening hours

We can clean early in the morning before you open, in the evening after you close, or at the weekend. Nothing gets in the way of your customers.

High streets and retail units

We clean independent shops and retail units on high streets and parades across Northwood, Ruislip, Harrow, Watford and the surrounding area.

Shop and retail cleaning: common questions

Can you clean before we open?

Yes. Most shops have us clean before opening or after closing, so the shop floor is never disrupted.

Do you clean shop windows?

Yes. We clean internal glass as standard, and we can add window cleaning to your regular clean.

Can you clean several times a week?

Yes. We clean daily, several times a week or weekly, depending on how busy your shop is.

How much does it cost?

Every building is different, so we do not have a price list. We visit for free, agree exactly what needs doing and how often, then send you a written quotation. There is no obligation, and we are not VAT registered, so there is no VAT to add.
