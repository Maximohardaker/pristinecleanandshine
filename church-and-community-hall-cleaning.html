Church and community hall cleaning

Halls and churches are busy places, with different groups in every day of the week. We time our cleans around your services, bookings and events, so the space is ready for whoever comes next.

Request a free quote Book a free site visit

How do you clean a church or community hall?

We start with a free visit to see the building and your booking pattern. We then agree a routine that fits between services, classes and events, and a list of what gets cleaned each time. We take care with old floors, wooden pews and furniture, and a supervisor checks the work.

What's included

We clean the whole building or just the parts you need. A typical routine includes:

  • Main hall floors
  • Naves, aisles and pews
  • Vestries, side rooms and meeting rooms
  • Stages
  • Kitchens
  • Toilets, with supplies topped up
  • Entrances, porches and foyers
  • Tables and chairs wiped down

Who we work with

Church administrators, parish and PCC officers, hall committees and the people who manage community buildings and their bookings.

After events

We can clean after weekend events and before the next booking, including Sunday services and Monday morning groups.

Church and community hall cleaning: common questions

Can you work around our bookings?

Yes. We agree cleaning times that fit around your services, classes and hire bookings.

Do you clean church interiors as well as halls?

Yes. We clean naves, aisles, pews, vestries and porches as well as church halls and community halls.

Can you clean at weekends?

Yes. We clean at weekends, early mornings and evenings as well as during the day.

How much does it cost?

Every building is different, so we do not have a price list. We visit for free, agree exactly what needs doing and how often, then send you a written quotation. There is no obligation, and we are not VAT registered, so there is no VAT to add.
