Deep cleaning for offices, shops and shared buildings

A thorough top-to-bottom clean that reaches the places a regular clean does not. Ideal before a new cleaning contract starts, or once or twice a year to reset a busy building.

What is a deep clean?

A deep clean goes further than a regular clean. As well as the usual surfaces and floors, we clean inside kitchen appliances and cupboards, descale washrooms, and clean skirting boards, doors, frames, radiators and high and low areas that build up dust over time. Contract customers can book one as an extra, and we also do one-off deep cleans.

What's included

We agree the full list with you before we start. A deep clean usually includes:

  • Kitchen deep clean: ovens, microwaves, fridges and cupboards inside and out
  • Washrooms descaled and fully cleaned
  • Skirting boards, doors and door frames
  • Radiators, light switches and sockets
  • High and low dusting
  • Internal glass and window cleaning
  • Floors thoroughly cleaned

When to book one

Before a regular contract starts, before or after a busy period, or once or twice a year to keep a building in top condition.

For contract customers

If we already clean for you, we can add an occasional deep clean to your contract.

Deep cleaning: common questions

How is a deep clean different from a regular clean?

A regular clean keeps a building clean day to day. A deep clean covers the areas a regular clean does not reach, such as inside appliances and cupboards, descaling, skirting boards, radiators and high-level dust.

Do you deep clean kitchens?

Yes. We clean inside ovens, microwaves, fridges and cupboards, as well as worktops, sinks and floors.

Can you deep clean before a regular contract starts?

Yes. It gives the building a fresh start, and our regular cleans then keep it at that standard.

How much does it cost?

Every building is different, so we do not have a price list. We visit for free, agree exactly what needs doing and how often, then send you a written quotation. There is no obligation, and we are not VAT registered, so there is no VAT to add.
